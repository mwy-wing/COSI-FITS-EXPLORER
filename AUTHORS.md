# Project contributors

COSI Explorer was developed through a collaboration between **WingYeung Ma** and **ChatGPT (OpenAI)**. At WingYeung Ma's request, the project records equal contributor credit:

| Contributor | Project credit | Contributions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WingYeung Ma | 50% | Project concept, COSI domain context, requirements, data interpretation, hands-on testing, and product decisions |
| ChatGPT (OpenAI AI assistant) | 50% | Assisted implementation, debugging, validation, interface work, packaging, and documentation |

WingYeung Ma maintains the repository. Collaborative commits use WingYeung Ma as author and record ChatGPT in a `Co-authored-by` trailer. GitHub's no-reply address is used for WingYeung Ma's commit metadata.

The 50/50 split records contributor credit, not a legal ownership allocation. ChatGPT is an AI assistant; this attribution does not make ChatGPT or OpenAI a legal co-owner. The existing MIT copyright notice naming WingYeung Ma is preserved. Dependencies retain their own licenses and notices.
